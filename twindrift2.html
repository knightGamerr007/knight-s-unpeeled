TWINDRIFT

Two cats. One set of controls. Left is right.

CHOOSE DIFFICULTY

MOVES 0PAR 0

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move both cats with one input. The orange cat mirrors left and right.
  • Park blue on the diamond and orange on the ring.
  • Walls stop each cat separately. Cats can never share a square.
  • Finish in par moves for 3 stars. A hint caps you at 2.
  • Keys: arrows or WASD, Z undo, R restart, H hint.
